Privacy Policy

App: Betanovo Mobile (Android) · Publisher: Aruxoc LLC · Last updated: 15 October 2026

This policy explains what the Betanovo Mobile app ("the app") does with information. The app is published by Aruxoc LLC ("we", "us").

Information stored on your device

The app lets you log basketball shooting sessions. It stores on your device only: the position and result of each shot you log, session names, notes and feel ratings you enter, session durations, and your preferences (theme, followed news topics, default session style and drill target). It also keeps the most recent news headlines so the news tab can be shown offline. This data stays in the app's private storage on your device. We do not receive it, and it is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

News headlines

The news tab downloads public sports headlines and thumbnail images from servers operated by ESPN. As with any internet request, these servers can see your device's IP address and standard technical request details. The app does not send your sessions, notes or other personal data with these requests. Opening a story takes you to the publisher's website in your browser, where the publisher's own terms and privacy policy apply.

Firebase Realtime Database

The app includes the Firebase Realtime Database software library from Google. It is used to read non-personal app configuration. The app does not write your sessions, shots, notes or any other content you create to Firebase. When the app connects to Firebase, Google receives technical information such as your IP address, as described in the Firebase privacy information. The app does not use Firebase Analytics, advertising identifiers, or crash-reporting services.

What we do not do

The app has no accounts, no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking. It does not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, and it does not sell or share personal information.

Your choices

You can edit or delete shots and sessions inside the app and change followed news topics in Settings. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app deletes all data the app keeps on your device.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this policy will be updated and the date above revised.
